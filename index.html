Research frame

Three questions, one cumulative artifact

The study moves from the reasons robots need to adapt, to the limits of classical BTs, and finally to the approaches proposed to enhance them.

RQ1

Adaptation needs

What adaptation needs characterize robotic systems?

RQ2

Classical BT support

To what extent can classical BT features address these needs, and what limitations emerge?

RQ3

BT enhancements

To what extent do enhancement approaches overcome those limitations, and what new limits remain?

Research design

Evidence is built and checked in cycles

Each cycle constructs or refines part of the artifact and concludes with internal or expert validation.

01

Problem exploration

Established work on uncertainty, self-adaptive systems, robotics, and the Multi-Annual Roadmap grounds the taxonomy.

  • Targeted literature searches
  • Six adaptation-need families
  • Co-author internal review
02

BT limitation analysis

A focused corpus is examined for classical BT features, limitations, and links to the needs identified in Cycle 1.

  • SciVal search in robotics
  • 31 selected primary studies
  • Traceable RQ artifacts
03

Technique characterization

The same corpus is classified by how approaches generate, extend, evolve, refine, or combine Behavior Trees.

  • Technical synthesis
  • Author validation
  • Questionnaire and interviews
