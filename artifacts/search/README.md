# Search artifacts

This directory contains two types of search material:

- `rq1-search-strings.docx`: the targeted literature-search strings used for RQ1.
- `questionnaire-recruitment-papers.csv`: 44 publication records used to identify potential authors to invite to the practitioner questionnaire.

## Questionnaire recruitment search

The recruitment source list was obtained through Scopus and focused on papers published from 2024 through June 2026 that mentioned Behavior Trees in their titles, abstracts, or keywords. The query was:

```text
("Behavior tree*" OR "Behaviour tree*") AND "robotic*" AND ("generation" OR "evolution" OR "refinement" OR "generate" OR "evolve")
```

The CSV contains the publication title, DOI, and source link. Author email addresses were extracted separately for sending invitations and are not included in the public replication package.
