Validation logic

Six hypotheses connect evidence to the RQs

H1–H3 are evaluated through author validation; H4–H6 through the questionnaire and semi-structured interviews.

HypothesisClaimEvidence sourcePrimary RQ
H1The mapping of primary studies to the four BT adaptation classes is consistent with the authors’ interpretation.Author validationRQ3
H2The limitations of classical BTs for adaptation are correctly identified in the scientific literature.Author validationRQ2
H3The mapping of primary studies to adaptation needs is consistent with the authors’ interpretation.Author validationRQ1 / RQ3
H4The classified adaptation needs are relevant in real robotic application scenarios.Questionnaire + interviewsRQ1
H5The identified adaptation needs are effectively addressed using BTs.Questionnaire + interviewsRQ2
H6Generation, extension, evolution, and refinement are suitable for at least some identified adaptation needs.Questionnaire + interviewsRQ3

Practitioner questionnaire

Instrument structure and public data

Data collection ran for four weeks in July 2026 and asked participants to judge needs, solution strategies, and the suitability of four BT enhancement classes.

01

Background

Robotics and BT experience, relationship to BTs, organization types, application domains, and professional role.

02

Adaptation needs

Relevance ratings for five grouped need families and judgments about whether adaptation belongs in classical BTs, extensions, updates, or external modules.

03

Technique suitability

Separate ratings for BT generation, extension, evolution, and refinement across each adaptation-need family.

Questionnaire PDF Questionnaire responses CSV

Quantitative analysis rule

Likert-scale responses are treated as ordinal data. Response distributions are reported, and “Insufficient expertise” is retained as a separate response rather than converted to a numeric value.

Qualitative interpretation rule

Open answers are used to explain ratings, disagreements, ambiguous categories, missing needs, and cases where a technique is suitable only under specific assumptions.

Semi-structured interviews

Six follow-up conversations

Interviewees were selected using self-declared expertise, the presence of critical or insightful questionnaire responses, and consent to follow up.
